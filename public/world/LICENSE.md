# World data

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
The compiled geographic packages are derivative databases. Source recipes are retained in
`public/recipes/expansion/` and `world-sources/` in the FLKVI repository.
St Thomas source attribution and SHA-256 are in `world-sources/st-thomas.source.json`.

Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen / Tilezen), including USGS 3DEP, NASA/USGS SRTM,
GMTED2010 and NOAA ETOPO1. [Full attribution](https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md).

Procedurally inferred buildings, furniture and camera positions are not surveyed observations.
